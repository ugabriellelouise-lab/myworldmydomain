About the Project

What it is: a personal blog site, "My World, My Domain," built for IT 111. It was planned as a group project and is being completed individually.

Purpose: to practice planning, designing, and building a multi-page website with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while giving my writing a home.

Goals: a clear navigation menu, a consistent color theme, readable blog pages, a working contact form, and a dark mode toggle.

Target users: classmates, instructors, and anyone who enjoys storytelling and student life blogs.
