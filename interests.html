Hobbies and Interests

Storytelling is my main one, but it is not the only thing that fills my time. I enjoy a lot of other things. I love, and I deeply love action movies like Mission: Impossible. Especially the insane stunts combined with what is surprisingly a nuanced writing

I play a lot of video games, but generally not the multiplayer ones. I'm talking ones that have a very fun gameplay and/or provide a very gripping storyline, whether it is climbing the blacklist and being the most wanted person in America with Need for Speed or navigating through a very hectic war and encountering dudes who can read your mind in Metal Gear. Or even, and especially my favorite, Persona. Which I frankly love the concept of basically having a second you in the form of a ghost and beat mosnters up. Though I occasionally play a lot of Fortnite (but only in Zero Build!)

I also recently a huge fan of motorsport. I watch a whole lot of Formula One. At least enough to out myself as someone who really enjoys the sport (despite its current problems...). I'm a huge fan of Fernando Alonso and Max Verstappen
